Everything you need to know about each product

Every product in Sonnet POS records the details below. Search the list by name or brand, or filter it by category. Batch numbers and expiry dates matter most for pharmacies, veterinary and agro-vet shops, and anyone selling food or drinks.

  • Product name and brand
  • Category
  • Batch number
  • Expiry date
  • Selling unit, such as bag, bottle, pack or carton
  • Price
  • Stock quantity
  • Low-stock threshold

Stock levels where you sell

The checkout screen shows how many of each product are left. Items at or below their low-stock level are shown in red, and items with no stock are marked OUT and cannot be sold.

Alerts on the dashboard

The dashboard counts your low-stock and out-of-stock items, next to today’s sales and outstanding customer credit. The reports list every low-stock product against its threshold.

  • Low-stock threshold set per product
  • Low-stock and out-of-stock counts
  • Low-stock list in the reports
Open the dashboard in the demo →

Stock updates with every sale

When a sale is completed, stock for each item goes down straight away, and the sale appears in the reports. There is no separate stock update to remember.
