Retail & beverage distribution

Beverage distribution operations

Industry
Retail and beverage distribution (single location)
Operational problem
Stock tracked in notebooks, paper credit records, and receipts written by hand at every sale.
System delivered
Retail POS with package-based pricing, credit limits and customer ledgers, plus physical stock audit counts.
Key workflows
Bottle / carton / unit package selling; cash and recorded Mobile Money payments; advance and credit checkout; low-stock alerts; physical stock counts.
Deployment model
Single-business system; cloud database with brief-offline transaction queuing, 80mm thermal receipt printing.
Business improvement
Designed to bring sales, stock and customer balances into one accountable system so the business can see where it stands at any moment.

Project anonymised pending client approval.

Veterinary & animal care

Veterinary practice management

Industry
Veterinary and animal care services
Operational problem
Credit and advance balances for animal owners tracked loosely; payments split across cash and later settlements with no central sales view.
System delivered
POS with a customer credit system, supplier records and transaction-ledger reporting for the practice.
Key workflows
Credit sales, advance and split payments, per-customer ledgers, supplier transactions, thermal receipts.
Deployment model
Local web deployment operated by the practice with staff cashier accounts.
Business improvement
Designed to give the practice a clear, running balance for every customer and supplier in one place.

Project anonymised pending client approval.

Agricultural & processing

Poultry and farm operations

Industry
Poultry and agricultural processing
Operational problem
Multiple sites of sales, expenses and supplier advances recorded separately, with no unified operational record.
System delivered
Custom management system with POS, expenses, supplier records and cashier dashboards for the operation.
Key workflows
Sales with discounts and backdating; expense categories; supplier transactions; credit collections; staff cashier dashboards.
Deployment model
Deployed for the farm operation with role-based cashier dashboards and thermal receipt printing.
Business improvement
Designed to connect daily sales and expenses into one set of reports the owner can review.
