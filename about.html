Sonnet Solutions Ghana Limited is a software company based in Labadi, Accra. We design, build and deploy POS and management systems — then stand behind them with training and support.

Who we are

Software builders, not software salesmen

We are a working software team. Every system we deploy has been designed, coded, tested and delivered by us — from the checkout screen to the reports the owner reads at the end of the day.

Our approach starts with how your business really works. We listen to cashiers, managers and owners, then shape a system around that workflow. If the right answer is a standard POS configured well, that is what we do. If it needs to be built, we build it.
