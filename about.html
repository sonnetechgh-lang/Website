About Sonnet Solutions

We build POS and management systems for businesses in Ghana.

Sonnet Solutions Ghana Limited is a software company on Montana Street, Amasaman, Accra. We design, build and deploy POS and management systems, then train your staff and support them after launch.

Software builders, not software sellers

Every system we deploy is designed, coded, tested and delivered by our own team, from the checkout screen to the reports an owner reads at the end of the day.

We start with how your business really works. We listen to cashiers, managers and owners, then shape the system around that workflow. If a standard POS configured well is the right answer, that is what we deliver. If something needs to be built, we build it.

You can try the same POS we deploy in our interactive demo, which uses fictional sample data.

How we work

From the first conversation to daily use.

  1. Discovery

    We review how your business sells, stocks and reports before anything is built or configured.

  2. Setup

    We configure the system, load your product catalogue and set up receipt printers and other hardware.

  3. Training

    Your staff are trained in person or remotely before you go live.

  4. Support

    Backups, fixes and help when you need it, by phone, WhatsApp or email.
