Systems we have delivered

Three deployments in Ghana, described without client names. We never publish invented results. For a reference you can call, ask us.

Beverage distribution

Industry
Retail and beverage distribution, single location
The problem
Stock tracked in notebooks, credit recorded on paper, and receipts written by hand at every sale.
What we built
Retail POS with package pricing, credit limits and customer ledgers, plus physical stock counts.
Key workflows
Bottle, carton and unit selling; cash and recorded Mobile Money; advance and credit checkout; low-stock alerts; stock counts.
Deployment
Cloud database with an offline sales queue and 80mm thermal receipt printing.

Client name withheld until the client approves publication.

Veterinary practice

Industry
Veterinary and animal care services
The problem
Credit and advance balances for animal owners tracked loosely, with payments split across cash and later settlements and no central view of sales.
What we built
POS with a customer credit system, supplier records and transaction-ledger reporting.
Key workflows
Credit sales, advance and split payments, customer ledgers, supplier transactions, thermal receipts.
Deployment
Local web deployment run by the practice, with staff cashier accounts.

Client name withheld until the client approves publication.
