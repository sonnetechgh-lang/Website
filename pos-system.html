Sonnet POS

A POS system built for how businesses in Ghana sell.

Sonnet POS takes you from product search to printed receipt in a few taps, with cash, Mobile Money, credit and advance payments, customer balances and daily reports in the same system.

Screens from the Sonnet POS demo. All data is fictional.

Fast, clear checkout

Cashiers search by name or filter by category, then tap product cards to build the sale. Each card shows the price and how many are left, so nobody sells stock that is not there.

  • Search and category filters
  • Stock level on every product card
  • Out-of-stock items cannot be added
  • Discount per sale, shown on the receipt
Try a sale in the demo →

Cash, Mobile Money, credit and advance

Every sale records how it was paid. Mobile Money sales keep the payment reference, credit sales add to the customer’s balance, and advance payments are drawn down as the customer buys.

  • Cash payments
  • Mobile Money with a payment reference
  • Credit sales, with a credit limit checked at checkout
  • Advance balances paid in and used later

Customer accounts and balances

Choose a walk-in customer or a saved account. The checkout shows what the customer already owes before you sell to them on credit.

  • Walk-in or saved customer on every sale
  • Credit balance shown when you pick the customer
  • Outstanding credit totalled on the dashboard

80mm thermal receipts

Each completed sale produces a receipt sized for 80mm thermal printers, with your business details, receipt number, cashier, customer, items and payment method.

Reports you can act on

See transaction counts, revenue, average sale value, a sales summary, best sellers and your payment mix. Every completed sale appears in the reports straight away.

Open the reports in the demo →

Works on desktop, tablet and phone

Sonnet POS runs in a web browser. The layout adapts to smaller screens, so owners and managers can check sales and stock from a phone.
