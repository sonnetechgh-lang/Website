Accountability

Staff roles and clear record-keeping

Staff sign in with their own accounts. Roles decide what each person can do, and every sale, adjustment and payment carries the name of the person who performed it.

  • Cashier and manager roles per deployment
  • An audit trail for every action
  • Cashier dashboards focused on daily work
Visibility

Reports you can answer questions with

Sales totals, best sellers, low stock, credit balances, payment mix and stock movement — the kind of numbers owners and managers actually ask for at the end of the day.

  • Daily sales and transaction summaries
  • Best and slow-moving products
  • Low-stock and credit-collection lists
  • CSV export in supported deployments
Reliability

Cloud or local, with brief-offline queuing

Deploy in the cloud or on your own premises. Where a deployment needs it, sales queue locally when the network dips and synchronise when the connection returns — so a power or data outage does not stop the till.
