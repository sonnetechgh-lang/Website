Questions about Sonnet POS

Short answers about how the system works. For anything else, book a walkthrough.

What is Sonnet POS?

Sonnet POS is a point-of-sale and management system from Sonnet Solutions Ghana Limited. It handles sales and checkout, products and stock, customer credit and business reports in one system. You can try it in the free interactive demo, which uses fictional sample data.

Does Sonnet POS accept Mobile Money?

Yes. A sale can be paid by cash, Mobile Money (MoMo), credit or a customer's advance balance. Mobile Money payments can include a payment reference, and every payment method shows up in the reports.

Can Sonnet POS print receipts?

Yes. Every completed sale produces a receipt sized for 80mm thermal receipt printers.

Can Sonnet POS track stock levels, batches and expiry dates?

Yes. Each product records its category, batch number, expiry date, price and stock quantity. Stock goes down automatically with every sale, and items that are running low or out of stock are flagged.

Can customers buy on credit?

Yes. You can save customer accounts, sell to them on credit or against an advance payment, and see outstanding credit balances on the dashboard.

Does Sonnet POS support several branches or staff accounts?

Staff access, customer workflows and multi-branch management are available on request for custom deployments.

Pricing depends on your business and the setup you need. Book a free walkthrough and we will recommend a setup and give you a quote.
